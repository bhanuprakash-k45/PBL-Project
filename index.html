A few things worth knowing

Clear answers before you book.

How far in advance can I book?

Book up to 90 days ahead. Availability is shown in IST and confirmed after approval and payment.

What is included in the price?

Hall rental is hourly. Equipment, cleaning, 18% tax and a refundable security deposit are itemized before you submit.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Submit a request from My Bookings. Refunds depend on notice period; the policy is visible before you confirm.

What if my preferred time is unavailable?

Join the waitlist. If a slot opens, eligible members receive a time-limited invitation to book.
